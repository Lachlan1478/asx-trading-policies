 
20 March 2023 
 
Adoption of New Securities Trading Policy 
 
The Board of Tivan Limited (ASX: TVN) (“Tivan” or the “Company”) is pleased to advise that a new Securities 
Trading Policy has been adopted for Tivan. 
The aim of the new Securities Trading Policy is to ensure the Company’s compliance with ASX requirements 
and align with market best practice. 
The new policy can be found at: https://tivan.com.au/corporate/corporate-governance/ 
 
This announcement has been approved by the Board of the Company.
