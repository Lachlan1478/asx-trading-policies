SECURITIES TRADING POLICY: CLOSED PERIODS 
 
 
In order to align the existing policy regarding closed periods to that of its major 
shareholder, the period during which Directors and Employees must not trade in 
group and company securities has with immediate effect been amended from: 
 
1 June until the business day after the release of the full year results, and 
1 December until the business day after the release of the half yearly results 
 
To: 
 
1 July until the business day after the releases of the full year results,  
1 January until the business day after the release of the half yearly results
