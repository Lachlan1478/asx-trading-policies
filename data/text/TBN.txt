Tamboran Resources Corporation   
Securities Trading Policy 
1.1 
Purpose 
(a) 
The Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) (Corporations Act) prohibits the trading in shares, 
options, debentures (including convertible notes) and other securities (securities) of 
a company by any person who is in possession of price sensitive information 
regarding that company that is not generally available.  The Corporations Act: 
(i) 
imposes substantial penalties on persons who breach those provisions; and  
(ii) 
applies to the extent of any inconsistency between it and this policy.  
(b) 
This policy regulates dealings by directors and officers of Tamboran Resources 
Corporation (Tamboran or the Company) and other designated persons, in 
securities in Tamboran about which they acquire Inside Information through their 
position or dealings with Tamboran. 
(c) 
This policy is not designed to prohibit Tamboran Persons from selling or investing in 
Tamboran securities, but does recognise that there may be times when directors, 
officers or certain employees cannot or should not sell or invest in Tamboran 
securities.

1.3 
General prohibition on insider trading 
(a) 
No Tamboran Person may, while in possession of Inside Information concerning 
Tamboran, in breach of the Corporations Act:  
(i) 
buy or sell any Tamboran securities at any time;  
(ii) 
procure another person to deal in Tamboran securities in any way; or  
(iii) 
pass on any Inside Information to another person for that person’s own 
personal gain by dealing in Tamboran securities in any way. 
(b) 
All Tamboran Persons are prohibited from dealing in the securities of outside 
companies about which they acquire Inside Information through their position with 
Tamboran. 
(c) 
The requirements imposed by this policy are in addition to any legal prohibitions on 
insider trading.  Trading in Tamboran securities is prohibited at any time by a director 
or a Tamboran Person if that person possesses Inside Information.  
1.4 
Inside Information 
A Tamboran Person is responsible for assessing whether they possess “Inside Information”. 
This occurs where: 
(a) 
the person possesses information that is not generally available to the public and, if 
the information were generally available, a reasonable person would expect it to have 
a material effect on the price or value of Tamboran’s securities (or a decision whether 
or not to trade in them); and 
(b) 
the person knows, or ought reasonably to know, that the information is not generally 
available and, if it were generally available, a reasonable person would expect it to 
have a material effect on the price or value of Tamboran’s securities. 
A reasonable person would be taken to expect information to have a material effect on the 
price or value of securities if the information would, or would be likely to, influence a person 
who commonly invests in securities to either deal or not deal in securities in any way.  Inside 
Information in relation to the securities of outside companies has the same meaning for the 
purposes of this policy, except that references to “Tamboran’s securities” should be read as 
references to the securities of the outside company.

1.5 
Blackout Periods 
(a) 
Tamboran Persons, subject to sections 1.7 and 1.12, may not buy or sell Tamboran 
securities during a Blackout Period.  
(b) 
“Blackout Periods” are times when Tamboran Persons must not deal in the 
Company’s securities. 
The following are mandated Blackout Periods: 
(i) 
to the extent that the Company is required to report on a quarterly basis 
pursuant to the ASX Listing Rules, the period commencing one week 
immediately preceding the release of the Company’s quarterly report and 
ending after the release of the Company’s quarterly report; 
(ii) 
the period commencing four weeks immediately preceding the release of the 
Company’s half-year report and ending after the release of the Company’s 
half-year report;  
(iii) 
the period commencing four weeks immediately preceding the release of the 
Company’s full-year report and ending after the release of the Company’s 
full-year report; and 
(iv) 
any other period that the Board specifies from time to time. 
During Blackout Periods Tamboran Persons must not deal in any of the Company's financial 
products or securities, or in any securities related to them.

1.7 
Exceptional circumstances 
(a) 
In exceptional circumstances the Authorising Officer, has discretion to approve 
dealings in Tamboran securities during a Blackout Period, or other dealings that 
would otherwise be prohibited by this policy.  Any approval given under this 
section 1.7(a), must be provided by electronic delivery via email.  The notification 
requirements still apply. 
(b) 
What constitutes “exceptional circumstances” will be assessed on a case-by-case 
basis within the absolute discretion of the Board, and may include, without limitation, 
severe financial hardship or a requirement to comply with a court order or court 
enforceable undertaking.  
1.8 
Company secretary to maintain records 
The Company Secretary will maintain a copy of: 
(a) 
all requests for an approval to deal in Tamboran’s securities submitted by a 
Tamboran Person; and 
(b) 
details of all dealings in Tamboran’s securities made by a Tamboran Person. 
(c) 
Provide upcoming blackout dates to the Board and management 
1.9 
No speculative trading 
Under no circumstances should Tamboran Persons engage in short-term or speculative 
trading in Tamboran securities.  This prohibition includes short term direct dealing in 
Tamboran securities as well as transactions in the derivative markets, involving exchange 
traded options, share warrants, contracts for difference, and other similar instruments, which 
are short term or speculative.

1.11 
No granting of security over Tamboran securities or entering into margin lending 
arrangements 
(a) 
Tamboran Persons may not at any time, directly or indirectly, grant any form of 
security (whether by way of charge, mortgage, pledge or otherwise) over any 
Tamboran securities which are unvested or subject to a holding lock, to secure any 
obligation of that Tamboran Person or any third party or enter into any margin lending 
arrangement involving Tamboran securities. 
(b) 
Unless paragraph (a) applies, Tamboran Persons may, directly or indirectly, grant any 
form of security (whether by way of charge, mortgage, pledge or otherwise) over any 
Tamboran securities, to secure any obligation of that Tamboran Person or any third 
party or enter into any margin lending arrangement involving Tamboran securities, 
with consent under paragraph 1.6. 
1.12 
Exemptions  
(a) 
Tamboran Persons may at any time: 
(i) 
trade Tamboran securities where the trading does not result in a change of 
beneficial interest in the securities; 
(ii) 
acquire securities under any director or employee security plan or through the 
exercise of options or performance rights under an option or performance 
rights plan or acquire, or agree to acquire, options or performance rights 
under an option or performance rights plan. However, any dealing in those 
securities remains subject to this policy and the provisions of the 
Corporations Act; 
(iii) 
transfer Tamboran securities already held into a self-managed 
superannuation fund or other saving scheme in which the restricted person is 
a beneficiary; 
(iv) 
acquire Tamboran’s ordinary shares by conversion of securities giving a right 
of conversion to Tamboran’s ordinary shares; 
(v) 
acquire Tamboran’s securities under a bonus issue made to all holders of 
securities of the same class; 
(vi) 
undertake to accept, or accept, a takeover offer; 
(vii) 
invest in, or trade in units of, a fund or other scheme (other than a scheme 
only investing in the securities of Tamboran) where the assets of the fund or 
other scheme are invested at the discretion of a third party;

1.13 
ASX Notifications  
(a) 
Tamboran must notify ASX within 5 business days after any change to a director’s 
relevant interest in Tamboran securities or a related body corporate of Tamboran, 
including whether the change occurred inside a Blackout Period and, if so, whether 
prior written clearance was provided. 
(b) 
To enable Tamboran to comply with the obligation set out in paragraph (a), a director 
must immediately (and no later than 3 business days after any relevant event) notify 
the Company Secretary in writing of the requisite information for the Company 
Secretary to make the necessary notifications to the Australian Securities and 
Investments Commission and ASX as required under the Corporations Act and ASX 
Listing Rules.  
(c) 
If Tamboran makes a material change to this trading policy, the amended trading 
policy will be provided to the ASX for release to the market within 5 business days of 
the material changes taking effect.
