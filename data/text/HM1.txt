1. 
PURPOSE & SCOPE 
1.1. 
This policy sets out the legal obligations and internal procedures relating to the 
buying and selling of Hearts and Minds Investments Limited ("the Company") and 
other securities. 
 
1.2. 
This policy applies to all Directors, Executives, the Company Secretary, and other 
persons designated as "Restricted Persons," including their associated parties 
(e.g. spouses, dependents, family trusts, and companies). 
 
1.3. 
All Restricted Persons must: 
1.3.1. 
Comply with the legal prohibitions against insider trading at all times;  
1.3.2. 
Ensure their associated parties also comply with this policy; and 
1.3.3. 
Follow the Company’s internal share trading procedures, including trading 
windows, blackout periods, and notification requirements. 
 
2. 
DEFINITIONS 
2.1. 
Inside Information: Information that is not generally available and, if it were, a 
reasonable person would expect it to have a material effect on the price or value 
of securities or on the trading activities of parties regularly trading in securities to 
with the information relations.  
 
2.2. 
Restricted Person: Includes all Directors, officers, employees, contractors, and 
their associated parties who may possess or have access to Inside Information. 
 
2.3. 
Securities: Includes shares, debentures, notes, options, other derivatives, interests 
in managed investment schemes, or other financial products or financial 
instruments traded on a financial market.  
 
2.4. 
Company Securities:  shares, debentures, notes, derivatives, or other instruments 
issued by or referable to HM1.

2.5. 
Blackout Period / Closed Period: A time when trading of HM1 securities by 
Restricted Persons is prohibited, such as the five business days prior to the 
announcement of a dividend, capital initiative, or other Board-designated 
periods. 
2.6. 
Associated Party: A person or entity whose trading activity is reasonably within 
the control or influence of a Restricted Person, including close family members 
and related entities. 
2.7. 
Exceptional Circumstances: Situations such as severe financial hardship or 
overriding legal or regulatory requirements, where written clearance to trade 
may be granted by the Chairperson. 
2.8. 
Dealing or Trading: Includes applying for, acquiring, disposing of, or agreeing to 
apply for, acquire, or dispose of any securities in the Company. 
2.9. 
Trading Window: A period designated by the Company (typically following the 
release of the Company’s NTA or other public disclosures) during which 
Restricted Persons may trade, subject to prior notification and clearance. 
 
3. LEGAL PROHIBITION ON INSIDER TRADING 
3.1. 
Under section 1043A of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), it is illegal for any person 
who possesses Inside Information to: 
• 
Buy or sell securities; 
• 
Procure another person to buy or sell securities; 
• 
Communicate the information to anyone else likely to trade or advise others to 
trade. 
3.2. 
These prohibitions apply regardless of how the information was obtained or whether 
trading occurs during a permitted trading window. 
3.3. 
Restricted Persons must keep confidential all Inside Information and must not 
disclose it in any way, including indirectly (e.g. through opinions or hints).

4. SECURITIES TRADING POLICY – RULES FOR RESTRICTED PERSONS IN RELATION TO HM1 
SECURITIES 
4.1. Board Discretion 
4.1.1. 
The Board retains absolute discretion to impose a trading embargo on 
Restricted Persons and/or their associated parties at any time, regardless of 
whether the trading window is open. 
4.2. Trading Windows and Blackout Periods 
4.2.1. 
Restricted Persons may only deal in Company securities during approved 
trading windows, unless exceptional circumstances apply. 
4.2.2. 
Trading of Company securities is generally permitted on the same day that 
the Company announces its Net Tangible Assets (NTA) or estimated NTA to 
the ASX. However, this exception does not apply if the NTA announcement 
occurs within five business days before the announcement of a dividend or 
capital management initiative, unless otherwise agreed by the Board. 
4.2.3. 
A period that is not a Trading Window is defined as a Blackout Period. 
4.2.4. 
Trading is prohibited during Blackout Periods unless: 
• 
The Chairperson provides written clearance; and 
• 
No Inside Information exists that may affect the trade 
4.3. Exceptional Circumstances 
4.3.1. 
In cases of severe financial hardship or other legal/regulatory obligations, the 
Chairperson may authorise a sale (but not purchase) of shares during a 
blackout period. Such authorisation must be in writing and specify a time 
limit. 
4.3.2. 
Requests must be submitted to: 
• 
The Chairperson (for Directors and the Managing Director); 
• 
The Managing Director or Company Secretary (for other employees 
and contractors).

5. HM1 SECURITIES - PERMITTED TRANSACTIONS 
The following dealings are exempt from this policy: 
• 
Participating in or allowing entitlements to lapse under rights issues, DRPs, or 
equal access buy-backs; 
• 
Accepting takeover offers or court-approved schemes; 
• 
Transfers where there is no change in beneficial ownership; 
• 
Trades under a law-imposed obligation; 
• 
Transfers to superannuation or savings schemes; 
• 
Trades in managed funds where investments are at third-party discretion. 
 
6. SECURITIES TRADING POLICY – OTHER SECUTITIES 
All employees, Directors, officers and contractors of HM1 must comply with the prohibition 
on insider trading set out in Clause 3 of this policy.  
All Restricted Persons must not knowingly trade in securities issued by companies that HM1 
is actively trading in at or around times that HM1 is conducting such trading.  
7. NOTIFICATION & ASX REPORTING REQUIREMENTS 
7.1. Directors must notify the Company Secretary of any trades in HM1 securities within 2 
business days. The Company must then report Director trades to the ASX as required 
by Listing Rule 3.19A. 
7.2. Notification of trades to ASX includes confirmation of whether the trades have taken 
place during a Blackout Period.   
7.3. All Restricted Persons may also be asked to confirm holdings or transactions during 
periodic compliance reviews. 
 
8. COMMUNICATIONS WITH THIRD PARTIES 
Restricted Persons must not disclose Inside Information to analysts, investors, or 
journalists. If such information is inadvertently disclosed, notify the Company 
Secretary immediately.

9. PROHIBTED PRACTICES 
9.1. Hedging 
Restricted Persons must not enter into any hedging arrangements over the 
Company’s securities (including options or rights) without prior approval of the Board. 
9.2. Margin Loans 
Restricted Persons must not include the Company’s securities in a margin lending 
arrangement without prior written approval from the Company without prior approval 
of the Board. 
9.3. Short Selling 
Restricted Persons must not engage in short selling of the Company’s securities under 
any circumstances. Short selling includes any transaction where a person seeks to 
profit from a decline in the Company’s share price, including through derivatives or 
other financial instruments. 
 
10. COMPLIANCE & BREACH CONSEQUENCES 
10.1. To assist the HM1 assess compliance in circumstances where HM1 reasonably believes 
there may be a breach of this policy, any Restricted Person must furnish HM1 with 
broker statements or activity statements in relation to securities accounts that the 
Restricted Person has control over if requested to do so. 
10.2. A breach of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination of 
employment or engagement. Breaches of insider trading laws may lead to criminal 
prosecution, substantial fines, and imprisonment. Any concerns or potential breaches 
must be reported to the Chairperson or Company Secretary. 
 
11. REVIEW & APPROVAL 
11.1. This policy will be reviewed at least annually and updated as required. Any changes 
require approval by the Board. 
11.2. This policy was last approved by the Board in November 2025.
