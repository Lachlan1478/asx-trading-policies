1. 
GENERAL PROVISIONS 
 
In this Policy referred to as: 
 
a.  Directors are Company Directors. 
b.  Special Relationship is an individual or body considered as having a special 
relationship with the Company if such individual: 
i. is an employee of the Company; 
ii. Has a relationship or submits a proposal in order to have a business relationship 
or professional activity with or for the interest of the Company or the 
Company’s subsidiaries. 
c. Material Information or Fact is information or an important and relevant fact 
regarding an event, circumstances, or a fact which may influence Securities price in 
the Stock Exchange. 
d. Material Non-public Information or Fact is an information not yet revealed by the 
Company to the public through corporate communication distribution lane or 
authorized official. 
e. Material Public Information or Fact is a Company’s information which has been 
announced to the public through capital market authority. 
f. Policy of this Company Securities Trading Policy will be renewed from time to 
time. 
g. Quiet Period is a period which begins from the time when the financial calendar 
quarterly performance report is available and ends when financial report is revealed 
to the public. 
h. Company Insiders include: 
i. commissioners, directors, or employees of the Company; 
ii. Major shareholders of the Company; 
iii. Individuals who, due to their position or profession or to their business relation 
with the Company, are allowed to gain Insider Information; or  
iv. Any parties which, within the past 6 (six) months, no longer become any Party 
referred to in item (a), (b), or (c) above. 
 
“Position” in the description of item (iii) refers to an office in government 
organizations, institutes, or bodies. 
“Business relation” in the description of item (iii) refers to work relation or 
partnership in business activity, including customer relation, supplier, contractor, 
client, and creditor. 
“Insider Information” in the description of item (iii) refers to Material Information 
or Fact possessed by Insider which is not yet available for the public.  Example of 
item (d): Mr. A resigns from his position as Director on 1 January.  However, Mr. A 
is still considered as an Insider until 30 June of the relevant year.

2.  
STATEMENT AND PURPOSE OF POLICY 
 
In order to ensure compliance with the Capital Market Law and Capital Market 
authority regulations in both Indonesia and Australia, the Company therefore establishes 
a Company Securities Trading Policy. 
 
This intention of this policy is to regulate trading of Company Securities at the time 
when an individual is in possession of an information which is material in characteristic 
and has not yet been published, tipping or disclosure of such information to outside 
party, and to prevent inappropriate trading and disclosure or tipping. 
 
Every trade of Company Securities conducted by Company Insider in possession of 
non-public information and material fact is considered as a violation against law.  Also 
falling into the category of violation against law is when a Company Insider provides 
non-public material information concerning the company to another person which 
allows such person to conduct trading of the Company’s Securities based on the 
information. 
 
This policy is also intended to prevent inappropriate behavior as a part of the company’s 
efforts in building and maintaining high integrity and ethical behavior which serve as 
upheld values in establishing the Company’s reputation. 
 
 
3.  
SCOPE OF POLICY 
 
a.  This policy applies for all Company Insiders as well as the consultants, brokers, and 
other people interacting with the company or the company’s units or subsidiaries, 
including sales agents and partners in joint ventures who gain or have access to 
Material Non-public Information or Fact concerning the company.

b. The parties included in the scope of this Policy are also responsible for ensuring the

4.  
COMPANY SECURITIES TRADING COMPLIANCE SUPERVISORY OFFICER 
 
The Company Securities Trading Compliance Supervisory Officer is the Senior Vice 
President Corporate Secretary, and in the case when the Corporate Secretary Officer is 
not present or unavailable, the Assistant Senior Manager Investor Relations shall then 
be authorized to confirm the compliance. 
 
Should Company Insiders be unsure whether the information they have are material 
and/or non-public, they are then required to consult the Corporate Secretary divisionin 
order to obtain advice before conducting a trade. 
 
In its duty to ensure compliance with this Policy, the Corporate Secretary division must: 
 
a. Administer and interpret this Policy and monitor as well as encourage compliance 
with all provisions and procedures in this Policy; 
b. Respond to all questions related to this Policy and the procedures therein. 
c. Decide and announce the special period of trading blackout in which there would be 
no Company Insider at all who may conduct trading of the company’s stocks; 
d. Socialize this Policy and other relevant materials to all employees and Company 
Insiders; 
e. Administer and encourage compliance with the laws and regulations applicable in the 
Indonesian and Australian stock exchanges where the Company registers its stocks; 
f. Revise this Policy when necessary in order to adapt to changes in laws and regulations 
concerning Company Securities trading by Company Insider (insider trading); 
g. Administer reporting process of Company Securities trading by Company Insider; 
h. Store company’s documents, both original and copies, required by this Policy and the 
procedures therein as well as all reports concerning insider trading required by the 
Indonesian Capital Market Supervisory Agency (BAPEPAM) or the Australian 
capital market authority agency.

5.  
PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES 
 
a.  Every Company Insider is prohibited to trade Company Securities when he/she is in 
possession of a Material Non-public Information or Fact concerning the company 
(unless those allowed according to the provisions below).  Despite whether or not 
there is an acceptable reason to sell or buy, when Company Insiders have Material 
Non-public Information or Fact, the Company Securities trading prohibition remains 
to apply. 
b. Every Company Insider is prohibited to trade Company Securities outside of the 
“trading windows” explained in article 6 below and Company Insider is not allowed 
to trade Company Securities during the trading blackout period set by Company 
Securities Trading Compliance Supervisory Officer. 
c. Every Company Insider is prohibited to disclose Material Non-public Information or 
Fact concerning the company to any outside party, including family members, 
analysts, individual investors, and members of investment communities as well as 
the mass media except required as a part of a routine task of employees, officers, 
members of Board of Directors or Board of Commissioners and members of relevant 
Committee or for the sake of a legal business purpose or after having been permitted 
by the Company Securities Trading Compliance Supervisory Officer. 
 
In every such case where such information is disclosed to outside party, the 
company shall take necessary measures in order to maintain the confidentiality of 
the relevant information including by asking the agreement of the outside party to 
comply with the provisions in this Policy as well as sign an agreement to guard 
information confidentiality (confidentiality agreement).  All questions from outside 
party concerning Material Non-public Information or Fact on the company must be 
directly forwarded to the Company Securities Trading Compliance Supervisory 
Officer. 
 
Information concerning parties which can be categorized into Exceptions for the 
sake of legal business purpose is specified in Company Information Disclosure 
Policies.  Exceptions for the sake of legal business purpose do not apply to selective 
disclosure to analysts, investors, media and other capital market professionals. 
d. Every Company Insider in possession of a Material Non-public Information or Fact 
is not allowed to provide recommendation for Company Securities trading. 
e. Every Company Insider is prohibited to conduct option or position trading in 
relation to the future (future price) of Company Securities such as put, call, or short 
sale. 
f. Every Company Insider is prohibited to give or cause anyone to transfer Company 
Securities during a period where they are not allowed to conduct trading.

g. Other than Investor Relations Officer, all Company Insiders are not allowed to 
participate in “chat room” on the internet or message boards which are related to 
company’s investment and stocks.  They are only allowed to observe passively. 
h. Every Company Insider in possession of Insider Information is prohibited to conduct 
purchase or sale of Company Securities; 
 
The prohibition for Company Insiders to conduct purchase or sale over Company 
Securities is based on a consideration that the position of being an insider requires 
them to put the Company’s interest first, or the stockholders’ interest as a whole, 
including not using Insider Information for their own interest or other Party’s 
interest. 
i. Every Company Insider in possession of Insider Information is prohibited to conduct 
purchase or sale of securities of other companies which conduct transaction with the 
Company. 
j. Every Company Insider is prohibited to influence other Party to conduct purchase or 
sale of the intended Company Securities; or extend Insider Information to any Party 
who the Company suspects will use such the information for conducting purchase or 
sale of Company Securities. 
k. Every Company Insider is prohibited to influence other Party to conduct purchase or 
sale of Company Securities even though the insider does not provide Insider 
Information to other Party, since this may encourage other Party to conduct purchase 
or sale Company Securities based on Insider Information. 
l. Company Insiders are prohibited to provide Insider Information to other Party who 
the Company suspects will use such information to conduct purchase or sale of 
Company Securities.  Therefore, Company Insiders have an obligation to be careful 
in spreading information in order for the Party receiving the information not to 
misuse the information for conducting purchase or sale of Company Securities. 
 
 
6.  
TRADING WINDOW AND BLACKOUT PERIODS 
 
a.  Trading Window for Company Insiders 
 
Company Insiders may conduct Company Securities trading after the company has 
disseminated information regarding quarterly, annual or end of year financial 
statements and this period shall end on the day of trading closing one day before a 
quarter or a semester ends, as long as they are not in possession of Material Non-
public Information or Fact concerning the company at that time or are not bound to a 
special trade blackout.

b. Trading during Trading Window is not carried out when a person possesses a 
Material Non-public Information or Fact. 
 
Every Company Insider in possession of Material Non-public Information or Fact 
concerning the company is not allowed to conduct Company Securities trading 
regardless of being in a trading window period.  A person who possesses such 
information may only conduct trading during trading window after the information 
has been announced to the public. 
 
c. No trading during Blackout Period 
Company Insiders are not allowed to trade Company Securities outside trading 
window period or certain blackout period set by the Company Securities Trading 
Compliance Supervisory Officer.  In addition to that, Company Insiders are not 
allowed to disclose to any outside party that a certain blackout period has been 
determined. 
 
Blackout period commences on 30 March, 29 June, 29 September, and 30 December 
every year and ends when the company disseminates information which previously 
is non-public information.  If the above commencing dates of Blackout Period fall 
on Sunday (weekend), the Blackout Period shall then commences at the closing time 
of the last trading day before the weekend. 
 
In order to ensure fair and symmetric distribution of information to all parties, the 
company always disseminates the information, which previously is non-public 
information, when the market has closed.   
 
Other than the periods stated above, the company also applies Blackout Period in 
certain situations where there occur changes which result in Material Information or 
Facts. 
 
 
7.  
PROCEDURES OF TRADING REPORTING  
 
The following procedures must be followed by Company Insiders conducting Company 
Securities trading: 
 
a. Due to the fact that there are certain times when the company considers it necessary to 
ask Company Insiders not to conduct trading because of a certain reason although 
such trading is carried out outside a routine Blackout Period as stated in Article 6, 
every trading of Company Securities by Company Insiders must be reported in 
advance to the Company Securities Trading Compliance Supervisory Officer. 
 
b. There shall be a notification complete with the reason of why the trading is carried out 
and a statement that the concerned party is not in the condition if having in possession 
any Material Non-public Information or Fact.

8.  
IMPLEMENTATION OF COMPANY SECURITIES TRADING RESTRICTION 
REGULATIONS 
 
a. Prohibitions and restrictions of trading stipulated in this Policy may not be applicable 
due to the existence of prohibitions and restrictions stipulated by higher law 
provisions and regulations. 
 
b. Every Company Insider who feels uncertain about other prohibitions or restrictions 
must ask the Company Securities Trading Compliance Supervisory Officer regarding 
the matter. 
 
 
9.  
SANCTIONS OVER VIOLATIONS  
 
The Company does not tolerate any practices which do not obey to this Policy. The 
Company shall conduct further process on violations of this Policy according to the 
company regulations and applicable laws and regulations. Sanctions which may be 
imposed on to such violations include: 
 
a. Legal Sanction 
 
According to Article 104 of the Indonesia Capital Market Law, a person who 
illegally takes advantage of company information and a person who illegally takes 
advantage of inside information obtained from Company Insider may be imposed by 
an imprisonment sanction for a maximum time of 10 (ten) years and a fine with a 
maximum amount of Rp. 15.000.000.000,00 (fifteen billion rupiahs).  The 
imprisonment and fine penalties also apply to any Party which, whether directly or 
indirectly, illegally influences another Party to violate the articles related to 
utilization of information.
